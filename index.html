About

I’m hands-on with the whole data workflow: extraction, cleaning, transformation, feature engineering and model integration, using Python, SQL, Pandas and PyTorch. I want to build reliable, production-minded data and ML solutions.

SafeCampus-AI

A real-time, privacy-preserving system that detects fights, screaming and other emergencies from live video and audio, with no cloud storage.

  1. CaptureLive video and audio streams from a webcam and microphone.
  2. PrepareFrame resizing and normalization, audio noise removal, feature extraction.
  3. DetectMobileViT reads video for violence and abnormal movement. Wav2Vec2 reads audio for screaming and emergency sounds.
  4. Fuse and alertA decision-fusion stage combines both signals and triggers automatic threat alerts.

About 90% F1-score with lightweight models, running in real time on a laptop, webcam and microphone with no cloud dependency.
